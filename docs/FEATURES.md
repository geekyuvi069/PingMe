# PingMe — Features (v2.5)

## 1. Pinging System
- Pings every 15 minutes (customizable).
- Chrome Extension handles initial ping with a 15-minute countdown.
- **Auto-Categorization**: Every response is instantly classified by Gemini AI into categories like Deep Work, Break, Admin, etc.
- **AI Nudges**: Receives proactive, personalized nudges from Gemini based on your daily progress and patterns.

---

## 2. Chrome Extension (Cyber-Noir UI)
The heart of the experience on Desktop.
- **4-Button Grid**: Log, Skip, Note, and Agenda in a sleek, monochrome high-tech interface.
- **Reading Hub**: In-app snippet reader for active books.
- **Progress Pips**: Header pips that fill as you hit today's reading goal.
- **Pause Mode**: Silence all pings for a set duration or until the next session.
- **Manual Sleep**: Toggle sleep mode manually if you're working outside your usual schedule.

---

## 3. Reading Habit System
- **Snippet-Based Reading**: Extracts small, digestible snippets from uploaded PDF/EPUB books.
- **Velocity Tracking**: Calculates your **7-day average reading speed** to provide accurate "Days to Finish" projections.
- **Scheduled Alarms**: Automatic native notifications at **9:00 AM** and **7:00 PM** to encourage consistent reading.
- **Book Selector**: Manage multiple active books and switch focus instantly.

---

## 4. AI & Intelligence Layer
- **Gemini 2.0 Flash**: Powers all categorization, daily nudges, and weekly cognitive insights.
- **Cognitive Summaries**: AI-generated notes in your email that synthesize your productivity trends.

---

## 5. Web Dashboard & Management
- **Timeline**: Visual log of your day with colored category badges.
- **Agenda & Tasks**: Checkbox-based tasks that **auto-carry forward** to the next day if incomplete.
- **Library Manager**: Upload and manage your PDF/EPUB library.
- **Settings**: Control sleep windows, ping intervals, and notification preferences.

---

## 6. Daily/Weekly Summaries
- **Rich Emails**: HTML reports with **inline SVG charts** for time breakdown and task completion.
- **Telegram Kickoff**: A morning message containing yesterday's carried-over tasks and a motivational kickoff.
